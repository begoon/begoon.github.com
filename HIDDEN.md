# Hidden posts

Posts listed here carry `unlisted: yes` (or `@unlisted: yes`) in their header.
The engine still publishes each of them at its original URL, but leaves them
out of the index pages, the search and the Atom feeds. Nothing is deleted: to
bring a post back, remove the flag and rebuild.

Posts are hidden in rounds, each reviewed and agreed separately.

Hidden so far: 45 Russian and 15 English posts, in 3 rounds.
