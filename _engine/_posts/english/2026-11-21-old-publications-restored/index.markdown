Old publications are back
=========================

articles, because the original pages at ACCU and The Pragmatic Bookshelf
are long gone. Recently I found the PDFs of those issues in my archives:
five issues of C Vu, Overload 107 and three issues of PragPub from 2011
and 2012.

The issues are now hosted here, and the links on the [Articles][] page
work again, each opening the magazine right at my article.
